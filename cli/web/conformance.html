provelog verifier conformance vectors

These are the frozen bundles every provelog verifier (Python provelog verify and the browser verifier) must reach the recorded verdict on. They live in vectors/v1/ with a documented manifest, so anyone can validate their own verifier against the same golden cases. The legacy-tagged vectors carry the pre-rename flightrecorder.bundle/1 format tag and domain labels on purpose: records stay verifiable forever.
